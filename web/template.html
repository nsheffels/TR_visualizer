Tandem-repeat pathogenicity visualizer

For known disease-associated tandem repeats: how far does each locus actually stretch in an ordinary population sample (max observed ÷ reference repeat count), and how much does it vary between people (population SD)? Background loci show how comparable non-disease repeats behave.

About the data

Tandem repeats are short DNA motifs (here 3–24 bp) repeated back-to-back. Most people carry a fairly stable number of copies at each locus; at a defined set of loci, a large expansion causes disease.

  • STRchive (Dashnow lab): disease loci with pathogenic thresholds, evidence tier, and inheritance. Only loci with evidence Moderate or better are included (52 of 63; 51 after excluding VWA1).
  • TRExplorer v2.0: genome-wide repeat-length statistics from population cohorts: HPRC256 (long-read assemblies, 256 individuals), TenK10K (short-read, larger, more prone to length errors), and AoU1027 (long-read, All of Us).
  • Background sample: non-disease loci drawn from the same catalog in the same CDS/UTR/intron/exon proportions and motif lengths as the disease set.
  • Fold-change = max repeat count observed in the cohort ÷ GRCh38 reference repeat count. It's the only scale disease and background loci share, since background loci have no pathogenic threshold.
Caveats
  • No pooling across cohorts. Combining cohorts would give loci covered by more or larger cohorts more chances to look extreme, independent of biology.
  • Max observed is not the pathogenic threshold. A rare full expansion essentially never shows up in a few hundred to ~1,000 people, so max observed at disease loci is usually far below pathogenic_min. That's expected. The detail box shows both.
  • Raw maximum is outlier-sensitive. For example, DAB1 in HPRC256 has a median of 13 repeats, a 99th percentile of 226, and a maximum of 1,003, all from a single outlier haplotype.
  • The background is structurally matched, not polymorphism-matched. Disease loci were partly discovered because they vary.
  • AoU1027 gaps are real. Several hard-to-genotype GC-rich polyalanine loci (FOXL2, HOXA13, ARX, ZIC2, SOX3, TBX1, PHOX2B, RUNX2, LRP12) have no AoU1027 data anywhere in the catalog. They drop out when AoU1027 is selected.
  • C9orf72's STRchive pathogenic_min (31) is the low end of a gray zone. Most clinical labs only call clear pathogenicity at ≥60–100+ repeats.
